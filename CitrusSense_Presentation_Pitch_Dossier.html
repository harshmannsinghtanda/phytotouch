3.0 Optics, Lighting Physics & Fungal Pathology Detection

Cross-Polarized Optical Illumination

The waxy flavedo surface of lemons causes severe specular highlight reflections under normal ambient bulbs, blinding conventional vision algorithms. CitrusSense employs a 5000K diffused ring light paired with cross-polarization, canceling surface glare and allowing camera sensors to read true subsurface carotenoid pigmentation.

UV-A 365nm Fungal Fluorescence

Early-stage *Penicillium* spores are invisible to the naked human eye during the first 18 hours of post-harvest infection. Under 365nm UV excitation, the damaged flavedo tissue fluoresces a distinct yellow-green tint due to the release of phytoalexin citropten, flagging rot before visible lesions form.
